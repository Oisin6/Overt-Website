Video for Dentists
& Clinics

Choosing a dentist or a clinic comes down to trust, and nothing builds trust faster than seeing real faces and real results. Overt Creative makes video for practices and clinics across Dublin and Leinster.

Patients don't book a clinic they've never heard of over one they've seen. Video is how a practice becomes familiar before someone ever walks in: the team, the space, the results, a friendly answer to the question they were nervous to ask. That familiarity is what turns a scroll into a booking.

Video that builds trust

Whether you're a dental practice, an aesthetic clinic, a physio or a private healthcare provider, the same thing wins patients: showing that you're professional, approachable and good at what you do. We make content that does exactly that, from treatment results to a simple welcome tour that takes the fear out of a first visit.

Answer the questions patients are Googling

Short, plain-English videos answering common questions do two jobs at once. They put a real, reassuring face to your clinic, and they quietly help you show up when people search for what you offer. It's some of the highest-value content a practice can have.
