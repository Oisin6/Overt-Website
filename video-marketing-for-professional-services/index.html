Video for Professional

Law, finance, HR and consultancy all sell the same thing: trust in your expertise. Overt Creative helps professional firms across Dublin and Leinster show that expertise with video that actually brings in clients.

If you're a solicitor, an accountant, a financial advisor or an HR consultant, your knowledge is the product. The problem is that knowledge is invisible on a website. Video makes it visible: it lets a potential client see how you think and how you explain things before they ever pick up the phone, and that's usually what makes them pick up the phone.

Make the expertise you already have work harder

You answer the same client questions all the time. Filmed properly, one clear answer to a common question becomes content that builds your reputation for months, positions you as the person who actually understands the problem, and does it while you sleep. It's the most efficient marketing a professional firm can do.

A brand that looks the part

Clients judge a firm on how it presents itself. A polished profile video and consistent, considered social content signal that you're established and serious, which matters just as much in professional services as the advice itself. We handle the planning, filming and editing so it looks right without eating your billable hours.
