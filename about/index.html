About Overt
Creative

A hands-on video marketing company for service businesses — making content that's built to convert, not just to look nice.

Overt Creative is a video marketing company run by Oisin Boyle. It exists for one reason: too many good local businesses are invisible online, not because they're not worth booking, but because nobody's showing people what they do.

One person, start to finish

When you work with Overt Creative, you deal directly with the person making the work — no account managers, no telephone game, no watered-down brief. That means the strategy, the shoot and the edit all come from the same place, and the content actually sounds like you.

Content built to convert

There's real craft behind everything we make — it's not a phone and good intentions. But looking good isn't the goal; getting you customers is. Every piece is planned around a result, so your content earns its keep instead of just sitting there.

Where we work

Based in the Midlands and working on location across Dublin and the wider Leinster region. Close enough to come to you, sharp enough to make it count.

The kind of businesses we help

Local service businesses — trades, salons, restaurants, clinics and the like — who know they should be doing more with video but don't have the time or the gear to do it well. That's the gap Overt Creative fills.
