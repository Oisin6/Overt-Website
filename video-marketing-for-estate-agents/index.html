Agents & Property

Property is a visual business, and video is how the best agents win both buyers and instructions. Overt Creative makes walkthrough and social video for estate agents across Dublin and Leinster.

Photos sell a house. Video sells the agent. A well-shot property walkthrough gets shared and saved far more than a set of stills, and it does something photos can't: it shows a seller exactly the kind of job you'd do marketing their home. That's how video wins you the next instruction, not just the current sale.

Property walkthroughs that get watched

We film listings properly, with the movement, light and pacing that make a property feel like somewhere people want to live. These are the videos that stop the scroll on Instagram and keep buyers on your listing longer, and they make your agency look a cut above the ones still posting photos only.

More than listings

The agents who really stand out are the ones people recognise. We help you build that with regular social content: market updates, just-sold clips, a bit of the face behind the brand. It keeps you front of mind for the sellers who'll be choosing an agent six months from now.
