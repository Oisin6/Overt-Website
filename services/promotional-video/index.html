A good promotional video does the selling for you — before a customer ever picks up the phone. Overt Creative plans, films and edits promo video that makes your business look the part and gives people a reason to book.

Brand & promo video, done properly

Whether it's a hero video for your homepage, an ad built to convert, or a brand film that shows what you're about, we handle it end to end. Proper planning, proper craft, and an edit that's built around the result — not just nice shots for the sake of it.

What you get

  • A clear concept — we agree the message and the goal before we roll.
  • On-location filming — your premises, your team, your work, shot well.
  • A polished edit — colour, sound, captions and pacing that lands.
  • Cut-downs included — shorter versions for ads and social from the same shoot.

Where you'll use it

One promo earns its keep across your website, your Meta and Google ads, your socials and your email — wherever a customer is deciding whether you're the one. It's the single asset that makes everything else look sharper.

Common Questions

Good to know

What can I use a promotional video for?

A single promo video works hard across your website homepage, paid ads on Meta and Google, your social profiles and email. We can also cut it down into shorter clips so one shoot fuels weeks of content.

How long does a promotional video take to produce?

Most promos run from a planning chat to a finished edit within a couple of weeks, depending on scope. We agree a timeline up front so you always know where things stand.
