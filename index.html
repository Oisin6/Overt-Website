How We Work

A simple process that gets out of your way

Most business owners don't have time to become a video producer on the side — and you shouldn't have to. Overt Creative is built to be the easy button: you keep running your business, we make the content that grows it.

1. Plan

We start with what you actually need — more enquiries, more bookings, more of the right customers — and map the content to it. No guesswork, no vanity views.

2. Shoot

We come to you and capture it properly. High-quality footage on location, shot to look sharp on a phone screen where your customers are scrolling.

3. Edit & deliver

You get finished, captioned, ready-to-post video back — cut for the platform and made to convert. You show up; we handle the rest.
