Your work speaks for itself, but only if people see it. Overt Creative makes video for construction firms, engineers and trades across Dublin and Leinster that turns finished jobs into new enquiries.

Construction and trades make some of the best video going, and most firms never film it. A build coming together, a driveway going from cracked to spotless, a fit-out finished to the millimetre: that's content people stop and watch.

Why it works

When a job is worth thousands, people want proof before they commit. A before and after does that in seconds, no sales pitch needed. It shows the standard of your work and the finish a customer can expect, which is exactly what wins the bigger, better-paid jobs.

What we film, across Dublin and Leinster

Builder, electrician, landscaper, engineering firm or fit-out contractor, we plan it around the jobs you want more of. Tell me what you're building and we'll make it look the part.
