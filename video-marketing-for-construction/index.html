Video for Construction
& Trades

Your work speaks for itself, but only if people see it. Overt Creative makes video for construction firms, engineers and trades across Dublin and Leinster that turns finished jobs into new enquiries.

Construction and trades are the easiest businesses in the world to make good video for, and the most likely to skip it. A build coming together, a driveway going from cracked to spotless, a fit-out finished to the millimetre: that's content people stop and watch. Most firms just never film it.

It works. One video we made for a powerwashing and garden maintenance business pulled in over 21,000 organic views, and another 13,000 on top from a €56 boost. That's the kind of local reach a single well-made video can bring. See the numbers.

Why video works for builders and trades

When someone's about to spend thousands on a job, they want proof you can do it. A before and after does that in fifteen seconds, no sales pitch needed. It shows the standard of your work, the tidiness of your sites and the kind of finish they can expect, which is exactly what wins the bigger, better-paid jobs.

What we film

We come to your sites and capture the work properly: progress on a build, transformations, drone shots of finished projects, the team doing what they do. Whether you're a builder, an electrician, a landscaper, an engineering firm or a fit-out contractor, we plan it around the jobs you actually want more of.

Built for Dublin and Leinster

We film on location across Dublin and the wider Leinster region, on your sites, around your schedule. Tell me what you're building and we'll make it look the part.
